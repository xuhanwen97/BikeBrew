How AU is scored. One standard drink (AU) is 10 g of pure alcohol: litres × ABV% × 0.789. A 375 ml can at 4.8% is 1.4 AU.
Jerseys. Yellow: most total AU. Green: highest average ABV. Polka dot: most total volume, the toughest climb. White: lowest average ABV. Average ABV is weighted by volume, so a pint counts more than a taster.
