How AU is scored. Alcohol units (AU) are fluid ounces × ABV%. A 12 oz beer at 5% is 60 AU.
Jerseys. Yellow: most total AU. Green: highest average ABV. Polka dot: most total fluid ounces, the toughest climb. White: lowest average ABV. Average ABV is weighted by volume, so a 16 oz pour counts four times as much as a 4 oz taster. Rows that can't be read (no name, or a volume or ABV that isn't a number) aren't counted in any stat.
