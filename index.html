Every row in the sheet, newest first, with the AU each scored.

How AU is scored. Alcohol units (AU) are fluid ounces × ABV%. A 12 oz beer at 5% is 60 AU. Drinks under 0.4% ABV (non-alcoholic) aren't counted in any stat: not AU, drinks, volume or average ABV.
Qualifying. A rider needs at least 3 counted drinks to hold a jersey or a place in the General Classification. Until then they're listed under "Not yet qualified".
Jerseys. Yellow: most total AU. Green: highest average ABV. Polka dot: most total fluid ounces, the toughest climb. White: lowest average ABV. Average ABV is weighted by volume, so a 16 oz pour counts four times as much as a 4 oz taster. Rows that can't be read (no name, or a volume or ABV that isn't a number) aren't counted in any stat.
